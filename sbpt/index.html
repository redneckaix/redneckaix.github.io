Privacy policy

Simple BP Tracker

A simple, private blood pressure log for Android.

Coming soon to Google Play

Add a reading in seconds. See where it falls, follow your trend over time, and share a file with your doctor when you choose to. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Everything stays on your device

The app does all its work on your phone. Your readings are never sent anywhere, because the app has no way to send them.

  • It does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot connect to a server.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync run by me.
  • No ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.
  • Charts, averages, categories and BMI are calculated on the phone.
  • Your data leaves the device only when you export a CSV and pick where to send it.

Details are in the privacy policy, including how Android's own backup treats app data if you have it turned on.

What it does

Log readings

Systolic, diastolic, optional pulse, date, time and a note.

Colour-coded categories

Every reading is classified, so you see at a glance where it sits.

Trends

Charts for 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 1 year or all time, with average and range.

BMI

Enter your weight and height in Settings and the app works out your BMI.

Export and import

Save your readings to CSV for your doctor, or bring them in from a CSV file. Duplicates are skipped.

Reminders

Optional daily reminders to take a measurement.

Your way

Dark, light or system theme. Metric or imperial units. Your preferred date format.

Six languages

English, Estonian, Spanish, French, Swedish and Finnish.
