Privacy Policy for Simple BP Tracker

Effective date: 30 September 2026

Simple BP Tracker ("the app") is a personal blood pressure log. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data the app stores

You may enter blood pressure and pulse readings, dates, times, notes, and optional profile details (age, weight, height). You may also set app preferences such as theme, language, date format, units and reminder times.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer cannot see it.

No network access

The app does not request the internet permission. It cannot send data to any server. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to show the reminder you set to take a measurement.
  • Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): used only to restore your reminders after the device restarts.

Exporting and importing

When you choose "Export to CSV", the app creates a file and opens the Android share sheet. You decide which app, if any, receives the file. From that point the file is handled by the app you choose and its own privacy policy applies.

When you choose "Import from CSV", the app reads only the file you select.

Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your device, your readings and settings may be included in your Google account backup, as for most apps. Google handles these backups under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer has no access to them. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes all data held by the app. Files you exported are separate and stay where you saved or shared them.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Medical disclaimer

The app is for personal tracking only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
